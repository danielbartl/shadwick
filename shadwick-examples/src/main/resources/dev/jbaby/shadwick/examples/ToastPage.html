Toasts

A direct notification from an Ajax callback or a full page render. No feedback message or extra markup required.

A little context

Five variants, an optional title and plain text. These close after five seconds. Hover, keyboard focus and a hidden tab pause the clock.

At your pace

Choose a duration or keep a message until it is dismissed. Alt+T reaches the newest notification; Escape closes the focused toast and returns focus after the last one.

Through an update

A toast is independent of the component that requested it. A full page render can show one too.

Full page toast

Alongside a dialog

The notifications move into the active modal so they remain visible, accessible and dismissible. Closing the dialog brings them back to the page.
