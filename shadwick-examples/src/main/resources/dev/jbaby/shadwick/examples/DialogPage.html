Dialog

A modal on the native <dialog> element, with a title, a description, the content and a footer.

Opened in the browser

new SwDialog("about", "About Shadwick", "About Shadwick"): the trigger label adds a button that opens it with an invoker command.

Opened over Ajax

SwButton.onClick("openDelete", "Delete project", dialog::open)

With a form

Its fields are validated on save; it stays open while one has an error. Try an empty name, or "admin".
