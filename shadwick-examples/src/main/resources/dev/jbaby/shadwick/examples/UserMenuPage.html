User menu

Your account, wherever you feel at home.

One account. Two places.

Open the avatar in the header, or your name at the foot of the sidebar. Both menus share the same account model, ordinary page links and a native Sign out form.

A welcome for every visitor

Switch to an anonymous visitor to see the Sign in link. Follow it to the demo entry page, then return to the workspace. This example keeps a demo identity in your session; it collects no credentials.

Right at your fingertips

Focus an account button and press Arrow Up to jump to Sign out, or Arrow Down to start with My account. The menu supports typeahead, Home, End, Tab and Escape.

Collapse the sidebar to icons to see initials and a name hint. On a phone, Escape closes the account menu first, then the navigation panel.
