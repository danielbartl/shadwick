Choosing a site

Fruit trees want sun for most of the day and a little shelter from the wind. A gentle slope lets cold air run off on a frosty night.

Preparing the soil

Loosen the ground a spade deep and work in compost. Heavy clay drains better with a raised bed.

Picking trees

Choose varieties that ripen at different times, and check which ones pollinate each other.

Planting

Plant in late autumn or early spring, with the graft above the soil. Water well and stake the young tree.

The first year

Keep the ground around the trunk free of grass, water in dry weeks and leave the pruning for the winter.
